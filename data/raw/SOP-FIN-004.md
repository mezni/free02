### 1. Policy Purpose

This policy outlines the standard procedures, timelines, and compliance requirements for generating, issuing, and managing customer billing statements across all Aether Wireless accounts.

### 2. Invoicing Cycle & Timelines

* **Billing Schedule:** Invoices are generated monthly on the 15th calendar day following the end of a subscriber's active billing cycle.
* **Payment Due Date:** Payment is due 20 calendar days from the invoice issuance date.
* **Grace Period:** A 5-day grace period is granted before accounts are categorized as delinquent.

### 3. Standard Line-Item Structure

Every generated invoice must contain the following itemized components:

* **Base Rate Charges:** Recurring fixed monthly plan rates charged one month in advance.
* **Usage Charges:** Variable charges (e.g., international roaming, overage fees) calculated in arrears.
* **Add-ons & Equipment:** Active monthly recurring insurance, device financing installments, or software add-ons.
* **Regulatory Fees & Taxes:** Statutorily required fees, including Federal Universal Service Fund (FUSF), state communications utility taxes, and emergency service (E911) surcharges.

### 4. Credit Approval & Authorization Matrix

| Approval Level | Credit Amount Range | Required Approval | Dispute Submission Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Support Agent) | $0.00 – $25.00 | Self-authorized goodwill credit | Within 60 days of statement date |
| Tier 2 (Billing Supervisor) | $25.01 – $200.00 | Billing Operations Supervisor | Within 60 days of statement date |
| Tier 3 (Executive) | $200.01+ | Finance Director | Within 60 days of statement date |

### 5. Adjustments, Credits, and Disputed Charges

* **Tier 1 Support Authorization:** Customer Support Representatives (CSRs) may issue one-time goodwill credits up to $25.00 per billing cycle without prior management approval.
* **Tier 2/Manager Authorization:** Billing adjustments between $25.01 and $200.00 require written approval from a Billing Operations Supervisor.
* **Executive Approval:** Adjustments exceeding $200.00 require approval from the Finance Director.
* **Dispute Timelines:** Customers must lodge billing disputes within 60 calendar days of the statement date. Charges undisputed after 60 days are deemed final.

### 6. Delinquency and Non-Payment Workflow

    [Day 0] Invoice Issued  --->  [Day 20] Due Date  --->  [Day 25] Late Fee Applied ($15.00)
                                                                 │
    [Day 60] Collections    <---  [Day 45] Service Suspended  <──────┘

* **Late Fees:** Accounts with unpaid balances after the grace period (Day 25) will incur a flat $15.00 late fee or 1.5% of the total overdue balance, whichever is higher.
* **Service Suspension:** Accounts reaching 45 days past due are subject to soft suspension (outgoing service restricted; emergency calls permitted).
* **Account Termination:** Accounts exceeding 60 days past due are subject to full service termination and routing to internal/external collections.

### 7. System Compliance & Audit

* All manual overrides or custom billing schedules must be logged in the primary billing engine with an associated ticket ID.
* The Finance Department conducts bi-monthly compliance audits to review credit approvals, tax remittance accuracy, and late fee waivers.
